# Lomo's Miscellaneous

**Temporal Analytics & SASSA Cycle Intelligence CRM & POS System**

A Progressive Web App (PWA) for small business operations, inventory management, and customer credit tracking. Built for offline-first operation with real-time SASSA grant cycle awareness.

---

## 🚀 Quick Install

### **On Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet)**

1. Open this URL in your mobile browser:
   ```
   https://clrbrandt.github.io/Lomo-s-Miscellaneous/
   ```

2. Wait for the page to load completely

3. Look for the **"Install"** prompt:
   - Usually appears at the bottom of the screen
   - Or tap the **menu icon** (⋮) → **Install app**

4. Tap **Install** → App installs on your home screen

5. Open the app icon and start using

---

## ✨ Features

### **POS & Sales**
- Log transactions with customer name, product, quantity, price
- Automatic SASSA cycle detection (grant windows, 15th, 20th, 25th, month-end)
- Payment type tracking (Cash vs. Line of Credit)
- Real-time transaction history

### **Inventory Management**
- Track stock levels for 5 products (ActionWork, AbdulUpDown, Snoek, ReservationA, ReservationB)
- Adjust prices and quantities on-the-fly
- Automatic restock alerts based on sales velocity
- Reset to defaults when needed

### **Analytics & Reporting**
- Total revenue and units sold dashboard
- Sales breakdown by SASSA cycle window
- Demand infographic (visual product performance)
- CSV export for Excel pivot table analysis
- Lay Day & Grant Liquidity tracking

### **Customer Relationship Management**
- Customer segmentation (New, Casual Buyer, Active Contributor, VIP Regular)
- Payment tracking (cash vs. credit)
- Credit ledger with eligibility scoring
- Total spend and visit count per customer

## 💾 Data & Offline

- **All data stored locally** on your device (localStorage)
- **Works completely offline** — no internet needed after first load
- **No cloud sync** — your data stays private
- **Persistent** — data survives app close and device restart

---

## 🔄 Syncing & Distribution

To share with multiple clients or devices:

1. Each user installs from: `https://clrbrandt.github.io/Lomo-s-Miscellaneous/`
2. Each device maintains **independent data** (no automatic sync)
3. To transfer data between devices:
   - Export analytics CSV from **Analytics** tab
   - Share manually or import to another system
